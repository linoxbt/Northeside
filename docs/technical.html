Northeside

Free weekly Premier League score predictions. Players seal their picks on-chain before kickoff from their own wallet; results come from two independent data providers and are published on-chain with a 12-hour challenge window; every matchweek has a public proof page.

The admin role is the owner's own wallet. Operator, results, attester, pauser and gas-drip keys are separate.

How a matchweek works

  1. Open. The operator creates the matchweek on-chain with the Merkle root of its fixtures, the lock time (first kickoff) and the hash of the scoring rules.
  2. Seal. The browser builds the picks and a random 32-byte salt and computes keccak256(abi.encode(chainId, contract, player, matchweekId, fixturesRoot, predictionsHash, salt)). The player's own wallet sends commit(); no relayer. Re-sealing is allowed until lock. New wallets get a small, rate-limited QIEV3 gas drip.
  3. Reveal. After lock, stored picks and salts are checked against the on-chain commitments.
  4. Results. Two providers (football-data.org and TheSportsDB) must agree; postponed games are void. Results, reveals and scores roots are proposed together and stay open to challenge for 12 hours.
  5. Finalize. The roots become final on-chain. Anyone can recompute a commitment in the browser; the proof page reads it straight from the chain.

Scoring: exact score 5, correct result 2. From matchweek 12, a sealed Banker (one game counts double) and a beat-the-crowd bonus (+1 / +2 / +3 when at most 50% / 25% / 10% of players called the result). The rules' hash is fixed per matchweek.

Identity: one person, one player

IdentityAdapter stores wallet → personHash from an attestation signed by the Northeside attester and submitted from the player's wallet. Tables and payouts count one person once.

Plan: QIE ID verification (which now includes QIE Pass) as the source of that attestation. The player approves a request in QIE Wallet, Northeside receives a signed credential with only the claims it needs (for example age_over_18), derives the person hash and issues the attestation. No contract change is needed.

Off-chain

  • Next.js app and API on Netlify; Postgres (Supabase) as an index of the chain. If they disagree, the chain wins.
  • A background worker every 5 minutes: index the chain, lock and reveal, fetch results, propose, finalize, post reward roots.
  • Sign-in with email (Privy, embedded wallet) or QIE Wallet directly.

Quality

  • 127 Foundry tests: unit, fuzz and invariant suites (solvency, accounting, one claim per person), deploy-script tests, golden vectors shared with the TypeScript code; Slither reviewed.
  • Package and API tests, plus an end-to-end run on a local chain driving the real UI: sign-in, seal from the player's wallet, lock, reveal, two-source results, finalize, proof verification.
  • Internal audits, with every finding and fix recorded.

Roadmap

  • QIE ID verification for the verified badge and host payouts.
  • $NSD rewards (built and tested, not deployed): fixed supply of 1,000,000, with 30% for players over the first 2-month season, paid weekly by finishing position to verified people only, one claim per person per matchweek, enforced on-chain. Launch waits for verification and legal review.
